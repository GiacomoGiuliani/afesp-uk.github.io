Hi, I started my PhD in September 2024, driven by an interest in marine heatwaves and their connections with extreme weather. My research focuses on the subseasonal-to-seasonal (S2S) predictability of marine heatwaves and associated compound events. My work explores how marine heatwaves develop and evolve, how predictable they are, and how they can influence the impacts of extreme weather events. I am interested in understanding the physical pathways connecting atmospheric and oceanic processes, marine heatwaves and associated hazards using causal approaches. Another aspect of my research considers how the choice of climatological baseline affects the identification and predictability of marine heatwaves.
  
My current work focuses on North Sea marine heatwaves, investigating whether forecast models capture these events and, importantly, whether they capture them for the right reasons.

My PhD is supervised by Ted Shepherd (University of Reading), Ségolène Berthou (Met Office), Magdalena Balmaseda (University of Reading; ECMWF) and Regina Rodrigues (Federal University of Santa Catarina, Brazil).
